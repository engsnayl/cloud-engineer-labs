# topic: keep our version during conflict
---SCENARIO---
During a merge conflict on `config.yaml`, you've decided to keep YOUR side of the conflict entirely (the version from the branch you were ON when you started the merge) and discard the incoming version.

What command?
---ACCEPT---
git checkout --ours config.yaml
git restore --source=HEAD --staged --worktree config.yaml
---CANONICAL---
git checkout --ours config.yaml
---EXPLAIN---
Then `git add config.yaml` to mark resolved. Note: during a REBASE, `--ours`
and `--theirs` are swapped because rebase replays your commits onto the other
side.
